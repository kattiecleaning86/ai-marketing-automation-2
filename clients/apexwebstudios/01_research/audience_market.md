# Apex Web Studios — Audience & Market Research

- **Prepared:** 2026-11-06
- **Labels:** **[F]** Fact (sourced) · **[O]** Observation · **[A]** Assumption / hypothesis — needs validation
- **Demographics:** no client CRM or analytics data is available yet, so no age, gender or income demographics are stated. Personas are built from the site's own services, package names, industries and published testimonials (B01, B04, B08).

## 1. Personas

### P1 — "Local Service Owner" (small business owner, 1–20 staff) [A]
- **Evidence it exists:** Starter/Growth web packages "for individuals and small businesses" (B04); "Local Services", "Healthcare & Wellness", "Real Estate & Construction" industries (B01); testimonials from a spa, a jewellery business and a historical society (B08).
- **Goals:** a credible site that brings in calls and bookings; being found on Google and maps; not overspending.
- **Pain points:** an outdated or slow DIY site; no time to manage it; confusion about SEO and AI search; fear of hidden costs.
- **Objections:** "Wix is cheaper"; "agencies disappear after launch"; "I got burned before". The site addresses this directly with "No surprises. No radio silence. No hidden costs." (B02).
- **Buying triggers:** a new year or a new season, a competitor's new site, a slow quarter, a launch or new location.
- **Platforms:** Facebook, Instagram [A — validate with analytics].
- **Language (from public testimonials, B08):** "growing our online presence", "flexible with changes", "looks amazing", "pricing is a little steep for me compared to others but Apex does great work".

### P2 — "Startup Founder" (pre-seed to Series A) [A]
- **Evidence:** MVP Development, SaaS Development, Mobile App, AI Agent services; meta description "startups, enterprises, and growing brands" (B01); app-cost blog posts (B09).
- **Goals:** ship an MVP fast, validate, raise; a technical partner they can trust.
- **Pain points:** cost and timeline uncertainty; scope creep; offshore quality fears.
- **Objections:** "Can a studio this broad go deep technically?"
- **Platforms:** LinkedIn, X [A].

### P3 — "Growing SMB Marketing Lead" (10–200 staff) [A]
- **Evidence:** SEO, PPC, SMM monthly retainers; Corporate Elite / E-commerce Growth packages (B04); testimonial: "our current services with them are SEO, PPC Social media marketing and management" (Ash Boucher, B08).
- **Goals:** pipeline and traffic growth; one accountable partner; reporting.
- **Pain points:** organic reach declining; AI search reducing clicks (TR4); internal bandwidth.
- **Platforms:** LinkedIn, Instagram.

### P4 — "Agency Partner" (white-label buyer) [A]
- **Evidence:** White Label Services page: "12+ Services Available", "100% White-Labeled Deliverables" (B03).
- **Platforms:** LinkedIn. Lower priority for December.
