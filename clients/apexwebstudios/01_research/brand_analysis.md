## 3. Personality and tone of voice

**[O]** Confident, direct, high-energy, outcome-focused. Uses short declaratives, triads, contrast structures ("We don't just X — we Y") and superlatives.

**Example lines (verbatim, B02/B03):**
1. "Great design means nothing without solid code behind it."
2. "No surprises. No radio silence. No hidden costs."
3. "Your next customer is already searching – we find exactly where you are invisible online and get you ranking where it counts."

**Social adaptation [A]:** keep the punch and confidence, and add a helpful, teaching layer. Swap superlatives ("sets the standard") for **demonstrated** expertise: show the fix, the checklist, the process. Avoid any guarantee language around rankings or results.
