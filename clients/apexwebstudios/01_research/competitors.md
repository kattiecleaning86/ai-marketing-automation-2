## 2. Opportunities (white space) — hypotheses

1. **Education for business owners, not designers.** The aspirational accounts teach designers; the local agencies seen post portfolio or keyword content. A "plain-English website and AI-search help for business owners" lane appears open.
2. **Pricing transparency as content.** Apex publishes all its prices (B04). Few competitors seen explain what each price tier actually buys.
3. **Accessibility expertise** around IDPD and the rise in ADA suits (TR1, TR2). No competitor seen focuses on it.
4. **AI-search readiness.** A timely topic where Apex sells both SEO and AI agents.
5. **Human proof.** Apex has a large body of on-site reviews and three video testimonials (B08, B01). Competitors seen don't use reviews on social.

## 3. Threats

- Price anchoring from $49–$67/month builder and subscription offers and from AI builders (TR6).
- Mixed reviews may surface in comments. Prepare reply guidelines (strategy §6).
- Name confusion with other "Apex Web Studios" businesses (Houston, South Africa, `theapexwebstudios.com`). Keep the branding and handle consistent.
