## 5. Funnel mapping

- **Awareness:** educational Reels and LinkedIn carousels (Pillar 1).
- **Consideration:** proof, behind-the-build, package explainers (Pillars 2–4).
- **Conversion:** free-call CTA posts; link in bio to `/contact-us` with UTM.
- **Loyalty:** community moments; reply-to-every-comment policy.

## 6. Engagement & community plan

- **Reply within 1 business day** to every comment and DM. Sprout Social reports 73% of consumers would switch brands if a brand doesn't respond on social (T30, search-located). Do not quote this publicly.
- **Comment prompts:** genuine questions ("Which of these fixes is on your list?"). **No engagement bait** such as "comment YES" (TR11).
- **Negative or price comments:** acknowledge, take it to DM, never argue. Link to the published pricing so people can compare tiers.
- **Accessibility posts:** answer general questions; for legal specifics, say "talk to a qualified legal professional".
- **Collaboration ideas (2027):** co-created posts with willing clients (e.g. the video-testimonial brands), with their written consent.

## 8. Measurement plan

- **Weekly:** reach, engagement and saves per post; comment and DM log.
- **Monthly (first week of January):** pillar performance, best hook, lead count by source, a recommendation for February.

**Test-and-learn hypotheses for December**
1. On LinkedIn, PDF carousels beat text + image on engagement rate (TR12).
2. On Instagram, Trial Reels with a "problem" hook beat a "list" hook on non-follower reach (TR9).
3. Package-explainer posts generate more DMs than generic "book a call" posts.
