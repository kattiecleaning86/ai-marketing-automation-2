## Regulatory notes
- **Sesame became the 9th major US food allergen** under the FASTER Act on January 1, 2023. For unpackaged retail bakery items, the FDA Food Code (a model code) calls for written allergen notification (S29, S30). Social posts must not imply products are allergen-free, and any allergen statements must match the shop's actual allergen information. _Fact_
- Brief: no health claims ("healthy", "guilt-free" are banned words). _Brief_

## Personas

### 1. "The Holiday Host" — Dana _(basis: Assumption informed by S22, S14)_
Hosting family or friends in late December and wants a centrepiece pie or loaf that feels special without baking it herself.
- **Goals:** a stress-free table; something with a story; reliable pickup timing
- **Pain points:** sold-out favourites; last-minute scrambling; not knowing what serves how many
- **Objections:** premium price; pickup hassle
- **Buying triggers:** clear deadlines, serving-size guidance, a gift-worthy look
- **Preferred platforms:** Instagram, Facebook
- **Content they consume:** "what to order" guides, behind-the-scenes Reels, reminders
- **Phrases:** "Did you order the pie yet?", "Bringing dessert this year"

### 2. "The Neighbourhood Regular" — Sam _(basis: Assumption informed by Brief)_
Walks or bikes by on weekends; coffee and a pastry are a ritual.
- **Goals:** a reliable treat, feeling known, discovering seasonal specials
- **Pain points:** favourite items selling out early; not knowing what's new
- **Objections:** none strong; the risk is becoming habitual and unengaged
- **Buying triggers:** new seasonal items, "out of the oven" timing, friendly faces
- **Preferred platforms:** Instagram (Stories, Reels)
- **Content they consume:** quick reels, polls, staff features
- **Phrases:** "The usual", "What's new this week?"

### 3. "The Weekend Food Explorer" — Priya _(basis: Assumption informed by S16, S24)_
Seeks out Portland's notable bakeries and shares finds.
- **Goals:** discovering craft and stories, finding photogenic moments, learning a technique
- **Pain points:** crowded "famous" spots; content that is all aesthetics and no substance
- **Objections:** "Is it worth the trip?"
- **Buying triggers:** process videos, specific signature items, timely events nearby
- **Preferred platforms:** Instagram, Facebook groups
- **Phrases:** "Worth the line", "Saving this for Saturday"

### 4. "The Local Professional / Office Orderer" — Marcus _(basis: Assumption)_
Orders treats for team meetings and holiday office gatherings; finds suppliers on LinkedIn and through local networks.
- **Goals:** easy ordering, reliability, supporting local business
- **Pain points:** lead times, quantities
- **Buying triggers:** clear ordering info, small-business story, reliability signals
- **Preferred platforms:** LinkedIn, Facebook
- **Phrases:** "Team breakfast", "Support local"
