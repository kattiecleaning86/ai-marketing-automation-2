## Competitor notes
### Ken's Artisan Bakery
- **Content angle (inferred from positioning):** heritage and craft authority, bread-first
- **Standout:** an established reputation; included in holiday roundups (S20)
- **Gap for us:** a more personal, neighbourly voice. Ken's has a legacy brand, while Harbor & Hearth can be the "new neighbour" with faces and humour. _(Assumption)_

### Little T American Baker
- **Content angle (inferred):** pastry craft; croissants with local ingredients (S21)
- **Gap for us:** "how it's made" education aimed at home cooks, such as how to store and revive a loaf. _(Assumption)_

### Lauretta Jean's
- **Content angle (inferred):** pies and seasonal preorder culture (S22)
- **Threat:** owns "holiday pie" mindshare in SE Portland
- **Gap for us:** pair pies with bread and holiday loaves as a "whole table" offer; communicate pickup logistics more clearly. _(Assumption)_

### Champagne Poetry Patisserie
- **Content angle (inferred):** an aesthetic, pastel, camera-first experience (S24)
- **Gap for us:** don't compete on pastel aesthetics. Compete on warmth, process and texture (flour, crust, steam). _(Assumption)_

### Oyatsupan Bakers
- **Content angle (inferred):** Japanese bakery specialities (S17)
- **Gap for us:** different category; mainly a reminder that Portland audiences reward a distinctive point of view.

## Opportunities (white space)
- **Process-first Reels** that show the slow craft (fermentation, shaping, scoring), built for watch time and shares (S5, secondary source).
- **Holiday logistics made easy:** a clear preorder deadline, a pickup guide and a "what serves how many" guide. Rival pie shops tell customers to order early (S22), which shows the demand is there.
- **People-led storytelling:** owner and head baker on camera _(Brief)_.
- **Useful tips that people save and share:** bread storage, reheating, making a holiday board.

## Threats
- Established competitors own "best bakery" lists (S16).
- Photogenic competitors raise the visual bar (S24).

## Formats to adapt (never copy)
- Short process Reels with on-screen captions
- Saveable "guide" carousels (storage, ordering, serving)
- Behind-the-counter staff features
- Local-event tie-in posts
