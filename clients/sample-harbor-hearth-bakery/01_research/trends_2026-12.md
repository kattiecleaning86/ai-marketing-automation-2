# Trend Research — Harbor & Hearth Bakery (FICTIONAL SAMPLE) — December 2026

_Researched 2026-11-06 with live web search. Each trend has a source; the source's age is noted. Secondary or blog sources are labelled as such._

## Instagram
### 1. Hashtags capped at 5 per post and Reel
- **Evidence:** Instagram is reducing the maximum from 30 to 5 hashtags per post and Reel, announced by Adam Mosseri in Dec 2025 (S1, S2, S3). About 11 months old; still the operative rule as far as search shows.
- **Relevance:** every Instagram caption in this calendar uses **at most 5** relevant hashtags. `tools/platform_rules.json` was updated to enforce this.
- **Pillar fit:** all · **Risk:** none · **Shelf life:** ongoing

### 2. Ranking rewards watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach
- **Evidence:** a secondary source citing Mosseri (Jan 2025) names watch time, likes per reach and sends (shares) per reach as the top signals. It reports that Sept 2026 commentary defended this ranking rather than replacing it (S5).
- **Relevance:** short process Reels that hold attention, with "send this to…" CTAs.
- **Pillar fit:** Bake Craft, Holiday Table · **Risk:** low · **Shelf life:** ongoing

### 3. Users can tune their Reels interests ("Your Algorithm")
- **Evidence:** the feature has expanded to all English-speaking users, letting them add or remove interest topics (S4).
- **Relevance:** consistent, clearly on-topic bakery and food content helps match people who pick baking or food interests. Avoid off-topic trend-jacking.
- **Pillar fit:** all · **Risk:** none · **Shelf life:** ongoing

## Facebook
### 4. Fresher Reels recommendations; all video publishes as Reels
- **Evidence:** Meta's Oct 2025 recommendation update surfaces more same-day reels (S8, S9). Since mid-2025, all Facebook videos publish in Reels format (S10; older than 12 months by December, so treated as background).
- **Relevance:** post short, timely Reels on Facebook (pickup reminders, "out of the oven now") and caption them for sound-off viewing.
- **Pillar fit:** Holiday Table, Behind the Counter · **Risk:** low · **Shelf life:** ongoing

## LinkedIn
### 5. Company pages get less organic reach; native video and personal profiles are favoured
- **Evidence:** secondary sources describe declining company-page reach, a preference for native content over external links, a push toward short vertical video, and a recommendation to amplify through employees' personal profiles (S6, S7). No official LinkedIn announcement was found.
- **Relevance:** LinkedIn posts are written as small-business owner stories, suitable for the owner to reshare from her personal profile. Keep links in comments rather than in the post. Use native photo or short vertical video.
- **Pillar fit:** Neighbourhood & Community, Behind the Counter · **Risk:** low · **Shelf life:** 2026

## Industry (bakery)
### 6. Indulgence, size variety and flavour innovation
- **Evidence:** Rise Baking's 2026 outlook (S11, S12). Technomic, as cited, finds impulse and indulgence drive cookie purchases (S13).
- **Relevance:** feature "small treat" sizes and indulgent seasonal bakes; use impulse moments such as "on your way to the market".
- **Pillar fit:** Holiday Table, Bake Craft · **Risk:** avoid health framing (brief) · **Shelf life:** 2026

### 7. Bakery as emotional comfort and reward
- **Evidence:** Mintel 2026 US bakery report summary (S14)
- **Relevance:** warm, comforting storytelling ("longest night, warmest oven"), with no health claims.
- **Pillar fit:** Neighbourhood & Community · **Risk:** none · **Shelf life:** 2026

## Seasonal and cultural moments (December 2026, Portland)
### 8. Holiday markets and weekend outings
- **Evidence:** The Nutcracker's Magical Marketplace (Saturdays Dec 5–19, S27); Old World Nordic Christmas Market (Dec 12–13, S28); Travel Portland December events (S26)
- **Relevance:** "Fuel up before the market" posts. Confirm event dates with the organisers.
- **Pillar fit:** Neighbourhood & Community · **Risk:** don't imply partnership or endorsement · **Shelf life:** Dec 2026

### 9. Holiday preorder culture
- **Evidence:** Portland pie shops tell customers to preorder early because pies sell out (S22)
- **Relevance:** deadline-driven preorder posts (deadline Dec 20).
- **Pillar fit:** Holiday Table & Offers · **Shelf life:** until Dec 20
