# Organic Social Media Strategy — Harbor & Hearth Bakery (FICTIONAL SAMPLE) — December 2026

## Summary
Launch Harbor & Hearth on Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn as **"your neighbourhood oven"**. The strategy has three parts:
- **Process content** that people watch to the end and save, to build awareness.
- **Clear, deadline-led holiday preorder content**, to convert that attention.
- **People-led community posts**, to build loyalty.

December's commercial job is to sell out holiday preorders by the Dec 20 deadline _(Brief)_. Its brand job is to stand apart from Portland's established and highly photogenic bakeries (S16, S24) by being warm, useful and personal.

**Positioning:** Slow-fermented bread and seasonal bakes from your neighbourhood oven, made by hand.

## Messaging pillars
- **Made slowly, by hand.** Proof: naturally leavened sourdough and hand-laminated pastry _(Brief)_.
- **Your holiday table, sorted.** Proof: preorder until Dec 20, pick up Dec 22–24 _(Brief)_.
- **A neighbour, not a chain.** Proof: owner and head baker on camera; local events _(Brief, S27)_.

## Content pillars
| Pillar | Mix | Funnel | Purpose | Example topics |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bake Craft & Education | 35% | Awareness | Show the craft and give useful tips that earn watch time and saves | Fermentation, shaping and scoring; storing and reviving bread |
| Holiday Table & Offers | 30% | Conversion | Turn attention into preorders with clear deadlines and logistics | Preorder open, what to order, last call, pickup guide |
| Neighbourhood & Community | 20% | Loyalty | Be the neighbourhood's warm third place; tie in to local moments | Holiday market weekends, solstice, thank-you posts |
| Behind the Counter | 15% | Consideration | Faces and stories that build trust and personality | Owner and baker features, a 4am oven-on day |

## Engagement & community plan
- Reply to every comment and DM within business hours; answer preorder questions directly.
- Use comment prompts that invite stories ("What's on your holiday table?") rather than engagement bait.
- Ask permission before resharing customer photos _(Brief: ask before reposting)_.
- Owner reshares LinkedIn posts from her personal profile and keeps the preorder link in the first comment (S6, S7).
- Pin the preorder post on Instagram and Facebook from Dec 1 to Dec 20.

## Hashtag strategy
Instagram now allows **5 hashtags per post** (S1–S3). Use 3–5 relevant tags, never filler such as #reels or #explore.
- **Branded:** #HarborAndHearth
- **Community:** #PortlandEats, #PDXFood
- **Niche:** #Sourdough, #HolidayPie, #Croissant, #BreadMaking
- **Broad:** #Bakery
- Facebook: 0–2 tags. LinkedIn: 2–4 tags (#SmallBusiness, #Portland, #ShopLocal).

**Social SEO keywords** (use naturally in captions and on-screen text): Portland bakery, sourdough bread, holiday pie preorder, croissants Portland, bread storage tips.

## Measurement plan
- Weekly: Reel retention and shares, carousel saves, link taps, preorder count (from the client).
- Dec 21: preorder sell-through review against capacity _(client data)_.
- Jan 5: month-1 baseline report that sets January targets.

## Test-and-learn hypotheses
1. Process Reels (shaping, scoring) beat finished-product photos on watch time and shares (S5).
2. "Send this to whoever's hosting" CTAs raise sends per reach (S5).
3. Saveable guide carousels (storage, ordering) earn more saves than single images.
4. On LinkedIn, owner-voiced text plus a native photo outperforms link posts (S6).
5. Tuesday 11:00 versus Thursday 17:30 on Instagram: compare reach per post.
